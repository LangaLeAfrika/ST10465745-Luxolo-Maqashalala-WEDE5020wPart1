Who We Are

The Eastern Cape Youth Skills Hub is a community-based, non-profit organisation dedicated to empowering young people.

Our mission is to support unemployed youth by improving their skills and increasing their chances of meaningful employment in the local economy.

Community Impact

We actively collaborate with community partners to create sustainable opportunities for youth development and economic growth within the Eastern Cape.

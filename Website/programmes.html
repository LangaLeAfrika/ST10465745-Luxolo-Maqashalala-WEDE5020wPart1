Frequently Asked Questions

What can I learn through these programmes?

Computer Literacy covers essential digital skills. Job Readiness focuses on CVs, interviews, and workplace communication. Entrepreneurship introduces starting and managing a small business.

How do I choose a programme?

Use the search above to explore topics that interest you. Compare the descriptions and choose a programme that supports your learning goals.

Where can I ask about dates, fees, and availability?

Visit our Contact page to ask for programme details before making arrangements.
